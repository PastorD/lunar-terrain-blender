This lookup ranks the smallest published DEM grid in the included catalogs. Footprints can contain missing pixels. In Blender, “Inspect Cached DEM Resolution” and the build report show the data actually loaded.

SLDEM2013 has a nominal 4096 pixels per degree grid (7.403 m north–south). Primary Kaguya TC input is about 10 m; MI/LOLA gap fill, interpolation and smoothing make its effective detail coarser and variable. Smallest grid does not always mean best geodetic quality. The USGS Kaguya TC v2 collection and NASA polar shape-from-shading products provide other quality choices; they are not automatically downloaded by this version. This map is not an exhaustive inventory of isolated research or surface-photogrammetry DEMs.

Catalog sources: LROC stereo footprints, NASA adjusted polar LOLA, JAXA SLDEM2013. The map works offline; source links need an internet connection.
