Chandrayaan-3
Surface photo references

LUNAR TERRAIN 1.5 · CHECKED 20 SEPTEMBER 2026 · ORIGINAL IMAGES: ISRO

Reviewed public release inventory, checked 20 September 2026. Six direct lander-reference assets, two additional 2026 paper figures, and seven supporting surface assets. Annotated versions, crops and stereo composites are grouped rather than counted as independent viewpoints. The registered PRADAN archive was not exhaustively enumerated; this is not a claim to contain every raw mission frame.

The preset uses grayscale Pragyan NavCam references for contrast and ground texture. Red/cyan anaglyph colours encode stereo, not soil colour. Text and links work offline; previews load from ISRO when online.

Additional views, research and archive

  • 2026 paper: additional surface stereo views
    Relia et al., Figures 2 and 3: processed NavCam views of the distant lander with detection overlays. Left/right panels form stereo pairs. Figure 5 is a derived point cloud, not a photograph.
  • ISRO mission gallery
    Primary releases for the images above.
  • 2024 buried-crater research update
    Additional distant Vikram and traverse images.
  • LPSC 2024 plume paper, Figure 5
    Repeats the familiar NavCam anaglyph; other figures are orbital or descent views.
  • LPSC 2025 Lander Imager observations
    The twelve selected images are orbital/pre-landing, excluded from the surface set.
  • Commons: Chandrayaan-3 on the Moon
    28 media entries reviewed: mirrors, crops, annotated versions, stereo derivatives, videos and orbital images; not 28 independent lander photographs.
  • PRADAN mission archive
    Register/sign in for the larger archive. NavCam raw/calibrated products: PNG + XML. Lander/Rover Imager products: JPG + XML.
  • PRADAN FAQ
    Date folders, product names, manuals and bulk download instructions.
  • ISRO science-data release
    Public PDS4 release, 23 August 2024, with registration for PRADAN access.

Excluded from surface references

R_img6–9: far-side orbital images; R_img11: descent imagery; ShivShakti_point: orbital landing-site image. Launch photographs, ground tests and artwork are excluded. Video frames are not counted as independent still photographs.
