# Lunar Terrain 1.1 — validation

Validated in Blender 4.5.3 LTS, 8 September 2026. These are implementation and visual checks, not a laboratory validation or a photometric inversion of mission photographs.

- **Existing-scene upgrade:** Apollo surface and outpost materials upgraded in place. Mesh datablock identities and active cameras retained. Appearance switching also preserves manually edited Sun rotation and energy.
- **Regional data:** the Apollo 17 and Chang’e-6 priors are read from the original 566 nm LROC product v1.1 cells. No polar extrapolation. Missing and unsupported parameter cells are rejected before applying partial changes. Original IMG checksum, conventions and cell values are in `lunar_terrain/data/hapke_provenance.json`.
- **Opposition sweep:** rendered linear ON/OFF ratios at phases 0°, 0.25°, 10°, 60° are 2.99272, 2.93335, 1.89070, 1.21067. Maximum relative disagreement with the scalar model is **0.00436%**. See `tests/photometry_validation.json` and `opposition_validation.csv`.
- **Roughness:** eight rendered combinations exercise both effective-angle branches, relative azimuths from 0° to 180°, and incidence up to 88°. Maximum relative radiance error is **0.000129%**. See `tests/roughness_results.json`.
- **Numerical limits:** 180 physically valid angle combinations through incidence/emergence 89.9° are finite and nonnegative. Reciprocity error is below 5×10⁻¹⁶; the zero-roughness result agrees with an independently written closed form within 6×10⁻¹⁶. Unlit hemispheres return zero. The optional CBOE zero-phase limit is checked.
- **Terrain and lifecycle:** registration, DEM units, exact-pole continuity, longitude wrapping, continuous mesh topology, cancellation, staging failure cleanup, active-camera preservation, zero populations and user-object preservation pass. The camera-visible solar disc follows the Sun; measured diameter is 6.573 pixels in a test expecting about 6.578 pixels. The world is black away from it. See `tests/results.json`.
- **Standalone scenes:** all three delivered scene files reopen and render without registering the add-on. Each has nine valid shader drivers and zero external file dependencies. See `tests/portability_results.json`.
- **Visual inspection:** Apollo surface (1100×1100), outpost (1800×1100), and Chang’e-6 surface (1280×960) were rendered with 48 Cycles samples and denoising and inspected. In the chosen foreground crop, Apollo's display luma median is 0.655, compared with approximately 0.639–0.675 in three of the supplied Apollo frames; the rockier fourth frame is darker. Chang’e's median is 0.407, within the broader range of the supplied color frames. These display statistics guide exposure only; geometry, lighting angles and photographic processing differ. See `tests/appearance_check_v1_1.json`.

The Apollo terrain/cameras are retained from version 1.0. The new Chang’e-6 example uses real LOLA 64 pixels/degree context (approximately 474 m posting); it does not claim a newly downloaded high-resolution SLDEM crop. Near-surface relief, rocks and small craters are synthetic. The existing adjusted south-pole 5 m DEM and Apollo 17 SLDEM pack remain bundled.

The shader is still a direct-Sun native-node approximation. Its angular treatment of indirect illumination, multiple lights and the finite solar disc remains approximate. The regional orbital fits do not establish ground-camera accuracy at every grazing angle.
